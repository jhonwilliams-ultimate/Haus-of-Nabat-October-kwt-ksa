Key Observations — October
🎯
Cost per conversation halved in Kuwait
Kuwait Sales fell from $26.79 to $13.66. Spend dropped 58% but conversations fell only 17%, so the smaller budget worked almost twice as hard.
🏆
Jahra is cheapest for the second month
$9.90 per conversation, with Ahmadi next at $11.61. Both were already efficient in September and have improved further.
🔴
Capital is still the weakest
$20.33 per conversation — more than double Jahra on the same creative. It improved from $34.11 but is still the clearest candidate for a budget shift.
⚠️
Frequency up, CTR down
Sales frequency rose from 3.93 to 4.36 and CTR fell from 1.62% to 1.17%. The smaller budget hit a smaller, more exposed audience. Watch this if spend stays low.
🇰🇼
Kuwait carried the account
Saudi Arabia spent only $115, so Kuwait took 97.1% of budget. Every account-level decline reflects markets switched off — not Kuwait performance, which improved on cost.
📉
Engagement stayed efficient but small
Cost per profile visit improved to $0.13 (from $0.15), but at 2,391 visits it is no longer delivering meaningful volume.

How to Read the Governorate Numbers
Method notes
  • Meta does not return Results by region. Reach, link clicks and spend are Meta's own measured figures. Conversations and profile visits per governorate are estimates — treat them as directionally accurate, not Meta-reported values.
  • How conversations were allocated: regional spend ÷ cost per messaging connection gives connections per governorate (they sum to exactly 324). The campaign's 262 reported conversations are then spread in proportion. Profile visits are spread in proportion to link clicks.
  • Connections vs conversations: connections count every thread opened (324); conversations count only new threads inside the 7-day window (262).
  • Reach shares are calculated against the governorate sum (Sales 65,285; Engagement 35,755), which is higher than campaign reach because Meta de-duplicates reach at campaign level. Unclassified delivery (17 reach, $0.28) is excluded.
  • Creative links open Meta ad previews and may expire over time.
